Marelle is a playful, rounded typeface with soft, chunky letterforms, designed as an alphabet-learning font for young children just starting to recognize and write their letters. Because it was made for early literacy rather than general-purpose writing, Marelle draws lowercase only — there is no capital set to fall back on, so typing in uppercase simply renders in lowercase instead.
