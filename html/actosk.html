Actosk is a sans-serif grotesque family built as a working system. Drawing its underlying structure from Monotype Corporation's Grotesque 215, Actosk extends that foundation across nine weights, Thin through Black, each paired with an italic. Actosk gives designers a full tonal range from quiet captions to commanding headlines. It is a dependable typeface for editorial, interface, and identity work alike.
